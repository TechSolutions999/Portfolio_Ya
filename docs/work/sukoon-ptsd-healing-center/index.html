Sukoon PTSD Healing Center

“At the Lowest Point, Toward a New Beginning”

Dead Sea, Amman — Jordan · Supervised by Dr. Feras Sharaf

Concept

Architecture as a Journey to Healing

By utilizing the Healing through Nature approach, Sukoon leverages its extraordinary location at the Dead Sea in Jordan. As the lowest point on Earth, the site offers unique therapeutic qualities, including oxygen-rich air and mineral-dense waters. Its position at the closest point to the Palestinian border also ensures symbolic resonance and ease of accessibility for those most affected by regional trauma.

The primary goal of Sukoon is to fill the existing healthcare gap by providing a facility that caters to the complex needs of PTSD patients, individuals with general mental health issues, and people with physical disabilities.

The objectives include the design of a barrier-free environment that promotes inclusivity and the integration of remarkable views and natural elements to reduce patient anxiety. By harmonizing the built environment with the therapeutic landscape of the Dead Sea, the project aims to establish a new architectural precedent for trauma-informed design in the region.

The Middle East has long been a region marked by conflict, leaving behind a profound need for specialized care that addresses both the visible and invisible scars of war. Sukoon is envisioned as a holistic healing center dedicated to survivors of Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder, offering a sanctuary for both mental and physical rehabilitation.
