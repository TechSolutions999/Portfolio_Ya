04 · 2024 · Design Studio 5

Agriculture Research Center

“Freedom and Rule”

University of Jordan, Amman · Supervised by Dr. Khalid Al Omari

Concept

Circle and square, curve and straight

The relation between circle and square, curve and straight, freedom and rule, can be found as well in the contrast between the two main architectural elements.

A circular courtyard with a living tree becomes the quiet heart of a precise rectangular massing, organizing laboratories, administration, and landscape into a single research organism.
