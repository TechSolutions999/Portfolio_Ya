02 · 2025 · Design Studio 6

Vault & Veil Boutique Hotel

“Bridging Time Through Space And Light”

Iraq Al Amir, Amman — Jordan · Supervised by Dr. Nancy Al Assaf

Concept

Layers of Memory: A Dialogue Between Past and Present

Set in the culturally rich landscape of Iraq Al-Amir, this boutique hotel emerges as a contemporary reinterpretation of the site's layered history. Rooted in the principles of adaptive reuse, the design preserves and integrates existing heritage structures—echoes of a vernacular past—within a bold architectural language of intersecting planes and voids.

Inspired by the timeless presence of the Qasr al-Abd and the carved caves nearby, the hotel acts as a spatial palimpsest: where new geometries hover, frame, and embrace the old.

This boutique hotel transforms a group of historic stone houses into a modern retreat. By preserving the original walls and introducing exposed steel and timber trusses, the design blends tradition with contemporary lightness. New roofs float above the old structures, while courtyards and stepped paths connect the rooms across the terraced site.
